# Five-minute demo (on a phone)

Open **https://finn-founding-f5fz.vercel.app** on a phone. If it says the server is waking up, wait 30–60 seconds.

1. **Today — a brief with an end.** Pick 24h / 3d / 7d. The funnel shows how much was filed vs how much matters. A
   must-read card may carry a dashed **Your network** line — e.g. a promoter-pledge filing at MTAR naming the contact
   best placed to explain it, and why. Tap **Got it** to clear items. Below: **Reason to reconnect** — someone you
   haven't spoken to whose former employer just filed.
2. **Feed — everything, ranked.** Switch to **30d**. Open Cipla's management change (new Global Chief Technical
   Operating Officer). The sheet shows who joined, their background *from the filing*, and **Your network**: Aditya Rao
   (Partner at BCG, reached through your classmate Neha) — solid lines are relationships you know, the dashed last step
   is an overlap FINN computed, with its confidence. Also try HDFC Bank's retirement and Cipla's new independent director.
3. **Person page.** Tap **Career & how you're connected**. Every career row quotes the filing sentence it came from;
   board seats are separated; "Worth asking about" notes come from the career itself.
4. **Research → TMPV (Tata Motors).** Snapshot, trajectory, balance sheet, cash quality from reported statements; then
   **Who to call**: the ex-Head of Treasury first, a supplier and a competitor contact next, with questions drawn from
   FINN's own reading of the numbers. Try typing `TATAMOTORS` in the address bar — it resolves to TMPV.
5. **Network tab.** Tap an example: *Who can connect me to Cipla's new chief technical operating officer?* —
   *Who previously worked at Tata Motors?* — *semiconductor manufacturing* — *Who haven't I spoken to in a while?* Tap a
   theme chip for the expertise map. **People in the news** lists everyone named in this month's filings; tap one to see
   how you'd reach them.
6. **Ask.** "Who in my network understands semiconductor manufacturing?", then "Compare the last four quarters of
   revenue for SUZLON", then "What are the most important developments in my portfolio today?" — the network, numbers and
   the brief from one box (voice works too).
7. **Alerts** (bell, top right). Change the price/volume thresholds; history re-scans instantly.

Everything about people says where it came from: *From filing*, *Demo data* (the network is fictional), *Inferred*.
